Before the shortlist

Give every possible find the same three checks.

CHECK 01 / IDENTITY

Is it the same item?

Compare the source item identifier, title, selected color and included components. A working URL can still lead to a changed listing.

CHECK 02 / SUITABILITY

Will it work for you?

Compare measurements, intended use and care requirements. “Looks right” and “fits right” are separate questions.

CHECK 03 / COMMITMENT

What are you agreeing to?

Read the current costs, return conditions and delivery restrictions. Resolve missing information before placing an order.

The reading room

Four studies in choosing more carefully.

01Footwear / Fit

Wheebuy sneaker finds: start with the shape of your foot

Decide whether you need a shoe for running, everyday walking or occasional wear. A similar silhouette does not establish equivalent cushioning, support or performance. For sneaker sizing, check the listing’s measurement method before comparing numbers: foot length, insole length and outsole length describe different things.

  • Length: use the seller’s stated size chart and confirm its units.
  • Width: ask about the toe box and midfoot if your usual fit is narrow or wide.
  • Variant: match the model and colorway across the title, photos and selected option.

When reviewing inspection photos, look for visible asymmetry, sole separation and the size label. An image of a branded shoe elsewhere on the web cannot confirm the construction of the item you are considering.

02Clothing / Proportion

Wheebuy clothing finds: measure the garment you already like

A familiar size letter is a weak comparison between unrelated listings. Lay a well-fitting garment flat and record its measurements. For a T-shirt, shirt or hoodie, useful checks include chest width, shoulder width and body length. For a jacket, add sleeve length and enough room for your usual layers.

Confirm whether the size chart reports a flat width or a full circumference. Pants need a waist measurement, rise and inseam; shorts also need an overall length check. A matching set may use one size label for both pieces, so inspect the top and bottom measurements separately.

  • Compare jersey print placement on the selected version, including the back.
  • Read fabric composition and care information rather than inferring them from shine or texture.
  • For underwear, check fabric, sizing and any hygiene-related return conditions before ordering.

03Headwear / Evidence

Headwear: a small label can hide a different fit

For a baseball cap, head circumference is only the starting point. Crown depth and brim shape affect how it sits, while the closure determines its adjustment range. Request a view of the back if the listing only shows the front and side.

Use a Wheebuy QC photo guide as a checklist for observable details: the selected color, stitching, closure and visible measurements. Seller photos present a product; inspection photos may show the item received. Check which kind of image you are viewing and whether it matches your order.

Neither a recognizable pattern nor neat stitching proves authenticity. If authenticity matters to your decision, assess the seller’s provenance and supporting evidence separately from a visual comparison.

04Accessories / Scale

Accessories: check scale, contact materials and closures

Close-up photographs can make a small accessory appear much larger than it is. Read jewelry dimensions in millimeters and compare them with something you already wear. For earrings, confirm the fastening type, post information and any available material disclosure, especially if you have sensitivities.

Scarves need a different comparison: width, length, fabric composition and edge finish. A flat photograph reveals the overall pattern; a worn photograph shows how much of it remains visible when folded. Neither view replaces measurements.

Keep unknown fields in your notes. An unspecified metal, missing scarf dimension or unclear clasp is a reason to ask a question rather than assume the detail. When comparing Wheebuy product links, record answers against the exact variant.

A useful cost comparison

Compare the delivered cost, not just the first number.

Put each applicable charge beside the item you are considering. Keep estimated amounts separate from confirmed quotes, and compare alternatives using the same destination and currency.

Item & local delivery
Check the selected option and any domestic delivery charge.
Service & payment
Confirm purchasing charges, conversion rates and payment fees.
International shipping
Check the route, packaging, chargeable weight and restrictions.
Destination charges
Check whether taxes, duties or handling fees may apply.
Chargeable weight can depend on actual weight or package dimensions under the selected service’s rules. Availability, transit estimates and import requirements depend on the item, route and destination; verify the current terms before paying. No price or delivery quote is offered on this page.
Direct answers

Questions before you click.

A concise starting point for using a spreadsheet, checking listing evidence and understanding where this guide sends you.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

The term commonly refers to an organized collection of product references used when researching purchases through Wheebuy. Individual spreadsheets differ in their sources, fields and maintenance. An entry is a starting point for checking a live listing.

Where do the links on this guide take me?

The ten category shortcuts open their named CAFANS category pages. All other navigation links open the CAFANS homepage. Brand photographs are editorial references, not links to a matching item or a Wheebuy checkout.

How should beginners use Wheebuy product links?

Keep the source URL or item identifier, select the exact color and size, then compare the current title, photos and options. Confirm that your chosen purchasing service accepts the item before paying.

What should I look for in QC photos?

Compare the selected option, visible measurements, color, seams, print placement and included components. Ask for a clearer view when an important detail is missing. Photos alone do not establish authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.

Does a working product link mean my size is available?

No. A page can remain accessible after a particular size or color has sold out or changed. Recheck the selected variant and availability on the current listing.

Does the listed item price include international delivery?

Do not assume it does. Check item cost, domestic delivery, purchasing-service charges, payment or currency-conversion costs, international shipping and any destination taxes or duties separately.

Is this an official Wheebuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent research guide. It does not claim affiliation with Wheebuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Hermès or Dior. Brand names and photographs identify editorial references and do not certify items found on linked websites.
